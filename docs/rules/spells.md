# Spellcasting

## Spellcasting (#336)

The casting engine is class-agnostic: spells are data, and a class's definition gives its spellcasting. The Cleric (#339) is the first playable class that casts; the Wizard brings its own spell list (D3). The test-only Test Caster and its fixed character, Sage (`src/test-caster-5e.ts`: Wis 17, spell attack +5, save DC 13 at level 1), exercise it in the engine tests, the scripted DM cases and trace replay.

- **Spells as data.** `src/spells-5e.ts` holds each spell from SRD 5.2: its level (0 for a cantrip), school, casting time (an action, a bonus action or a reaction), effect, damage type, saving throw, duration, concentration, cantrip growth and upcast rule. The engine reads only this data. The spells so far: Fire Bolt (ranged spell attack, 1d10 fire), Sacred Flame (Dexterity save, 1d8 radiant, none on a success), Shocking Grasp (melee spell attack, 1d8 lightning), Magic Missile (three darts of 1d4 + 1 force that always hit; one more dart per slot level above 1st), Inflict Wounds (Constitution save, 2d10 necrotic, half on a success; +1d10 per slot level above 1st), Cure Wounds (2d8 + the spellcasting modifier; +2d8 per slot level above 1st), Healing Word (a bonus action: 2d4 + the modifier; +2d4 per slot level above 1st), from #338 the area spells Burning Hands (a 15-foot cone: Dexterity save, 3d6 fire, half on a success; +1d6 per slot level above 1st), Shatter (2nd level, a 10-foot sphere: Constitution save, 3d8 thunder, half; +1d8 per slot level above 2nd) and Fireball (3rd level, a 20-foot sphere: Dexterity save, 8d6 fire, half; +1d6 per slot level above 3rd), and from #337 Bless (concentration, up to 1 minute: +1d4 to attack rolls and saving throws), Shield of Faith (a bonus action; concentration, up to 10 minutes: +2 AC), Mage Armor (8 hours: base AC 13 + Dexterity on a target wearing no armour) and Shield (a reaction to being hit: +5 AC until the start of the caster's next turn), from #339 Guidance, Resistance and Thaumaturgy (cantrips) and Guiding Bolt (below), and from #340 Ray of Frost and Chill Touch (cantrips), Chromatic Orb, Thunderwave and Sleep (below).
- **Known and prepared spells.** A caster's sheet records the cantrips it knows and the spells it has prepared (`spells`), its class's counts for its level (fewer only while a new level leaves some to choose, #341), each on the class's list, and each prepared spell of a level it has slots for and not one it always has prepared (the Life Domain's, #341). A class that casts nothing has none. Prepared spells change only between adventures, in the library (D8, #339; see The Cleric, level 1); a Wizard prepares only spells in its spellbook (#340), and a level-5 Wizard's Memorize Spell swaps one on a short rest for the rest of the adventure (#344).
- **Spell attack bonus and save DC.** A spell attack adds the proficiency bonus and the spellcasting ability modifier; a spell's save DC is 8 + both (SRD 5.2).
- **Cantrips.** A cantrip is cast at will, spending no slot. Its damage dice grow at character level 5 (Fire Bolt 2d10, Sacred Flame 2d8, Shocking Grasp 2d8).
- **Spell slots.** A class's data gives its slots of each level at each character level (the Test Caster has the SRD 5.2 full-caster table: 2 1st-level slots at level 1, up to 4, 3 and 2 of 1st, 2nd and 3rd level at level 5). A levelled spell spends one slot of its level or higher; a higher slot makes it stronger by its upcast rule. Slots are tracked as feature uses (`spell-slots-1` and on), so they last from fight to fight, a long rest restores them all, a short rest none, and a rest between adventures restores them all. The AI DM's character status reports them ("Spell slots: 1st 1 of 2 left").
- **Casting in a fight.** A spell whose casting time is an action takes the Magic action; a bonus-action spell takes the bonus action. Only one spell slot may be spent a turn (SRD 5.2): after a levelled spell, by action or bonus action, another is refused, though a cantrip is not. Action Surge's extra action can cast a cantrip, not a second levelled spell. A reaction spell (#337) is cast on an opponent's turn, so its slot isn't the turn's one.
- **Effects.** A _spell attack_ rolls d20 + the spell attack bonus against AC, like a weapon attack: a natural 20 is a critical hit (its dice doubled) and a natural 1 misses; advantage and disadvantage come from the same engine rules (Sap, Vex, Hide, Steady Aim, conditions). A ranged spell attack follows the ranged-weapon rule (D6): no disadvantage in round 1, disadvantage (Close combat) from round 2; a melee one has none. Spell attacks never deal Sneak Attack, which needs a weapon. A _save_ spell makes its target roll its saving throw against the caster's DC (a paralysed target fails a Dexterity save without a roll): a failure takes the damage, a success half of it (rounded down) or none, and no damage dice are rolled when a success takes none. Magic Missile's darts _always hit_, each rolling its own die. A _healing_ spell heals its dice + the spellcasting modifier, up to the maximum. Damage meets resistance, vulnerability and immunity like any damage, and radiant damage, or a critical spell attack, bypasses Undead Fortitude. A target a spell drops is defeated and its side checks morale, as for an attack.
- **The Cleric's spells (#339).**
  - **Guidance** (cantrip, concentration, up to 1 minute): the character's next ability check that the engine grades (a feature's, a door's, a trap's, a topic's or a search's check, or a parley) adds 1d4, rolled after the d20 and named on the card ("d20 9 + 3 + 3 (Guidance) = 15"); then Guidance ends ("Guidance ends on you: its die is spent."). It may be cast outside a fight, where checks are made. SRD 5.2 lets the caster choose one skill and adds the die to every check with it for the minute; without a clock the game gives the die to the next check, whatever its skill (owner decision). Its 1-minute band (D9) still applies: it ends with a fight, at a rest or with the adventure if no check comes first. A Stealth check to sneak or hide is not graded, so it doesn't spend Guidance.
  - **Resistance** (cantrip, concentration, up to 1 minute): cast with a damage type (acid, bludgeoning, cold, fire, lightning, necrotic, piercing, poison, radiant, slashing or thunder, as SRD 5.2 lists). Once a turn, when the target takes damage of that type from an attack, 1d4 comes off it (the weapon's damage first, else the rider's), before Uncanny Dodge's halving; the card says "less 3 (Resistance)". The bar offers it once for each such type the foes' attacks deal ("Resistance against piercing (cantrip)"), and only in a fight, as it lasts no longer than one; any other type, a type for another spell, or none is refused ("Choose the damage type Resistance resists: …"), and so is a type no opponent still in the fight deals with an attack or its rider, from the bar, the AI DM or the API alike ("Resistance resists only damage your opponents' attacks deal: slashing."). Chromatic Orb's types are not limited so.
  - **Thaumaturgy** (cantrip): flavour only. It is known and shown on the sheet ("flavour only: no effect in play"), never offered as a cast, and a cast of it is refused ("Thaumaturgy is flavour only: it has no effect in play."). The AI DM may describe its harmless wonders in words; they never change a check, a roll or an outcome.
  - **Guiding Bolt** (1st level): a ranged spell attack, 4d6 radiant, +1d6 per slot level above 1st. A hit on a target left standing gives the next attack roll against that target advantage ("Guiding Bolt"), whoever makes it, until the end of the caster's next turn; the attack spends it, hit or miss.
- **The Cleric's 2nd-level spells (#341).**
  - **Aid** (8 hours; a Life Domain spell): the character's maximum and current hit points rise by 5 (+5 per slot level above 2nd) until a long rest or the adventure's end; a long rest then restores hit points to the maximum without it. SRD 5.2's three targets are one without companions. Cast in or out of a fight; not again while it lasts.
  - **Lesser Restoration** (a bonus action; a Life Domain spell): ends the character's paralysis or poison, the first it has in that order (blinded and deafened don't exist here). A paralysed character can't cast it on itself, so in practice it cures poison. Refused when there is nothing to cure ("You have none of the conditions Lesser Restoration ends: paralysed, poisoned."). Conditions end with a fight, so it is cast only in one.
  - **Spiritual Weapon** (a bonus action, concentration, up to 1 minute): a melee spell attack as it is cast, 1d8 + the spellcasting modifier force (+1d8 per slot level above 2nd); then, on each later turn while it lasts, a bonus action attacks again with it. Moving the weapon is omitted (no positions).
  - **Hold Person** (concentration, up to 1 minute): only a humanoid ("Hold Person works only on a humanoid, and Zombie is an undead."); a Wisdom save or paralysed while the spell lasts, a save at the end of each of its turns ending it, and damage doesn't end it. A creature immune to paralysis succeeds without a roll. Its extra targets from a higher slot are omitted. Of the shipped bestiary, the Bandit, the Bandit Captain and the Warrior Veteran are humanoids.
  - **Protection from Poison** (1 hour, so until the next rest, D9): ends the character's poison, then gives resistance to poison damage (cancelling a vulnerability) and advantage on saving throws against being poisoned ("advantage: Protection from Poison").
  - **Prayer of Healing** (ten minutes to cast, so only outside a fight: "Prayer of Healing takes ten minutes to cast: only outside a fight."): heals 2d8 (+1d8 per slot level above 2nd) with no modifier, Disciple of Life's 2 + the slot level added, and gives a short rest's recovery of feature uses (one Channel Divinity use), without spending hit dice and without counting as one of the adventure's two short rests. Its target can't benefit from it again until a long rest ("You can't benefit from Prayer of Healing again until a long rest."). Like every healing spell it is refused at full hit points, even when its short rest would give a use back. SRD 5.2's five targets are one.
- **The Cleric's 3rd-level spells (#342).**
  - **Mass Healing Word** (a bonus action; a Life Domain spell from level 5): heals 2d4 + the spellcasting modifier (+1d4 per slot level above 3rd), with Disciple of Life's 2 + the slot level. SRD 5.2's six creatures are one without companions. Cast in or out of a fight.
  - **Spirit Guardians** (concentration, up to 10 minutes): an area spell catching up to three opponents (a 15-foot emanation, D4), chosen at casting. Each makes a Wisdom saving throw against one roll of 3d8 radiant (+1d8 per slot level above 3rd) as it is cast, taking it or half on a success; then, while the caster concentrates, each caught opponent still in the fight saves again at the end of each of its turns against a fresh roll. A creature makes the save only once a turn, which the engine's turns keep. The guardians end with the fight (owner's choice, not D9's next rest: they only strike the foes they caught). Halving the foes' speed is omitted (no positions), and the damage is always radiant (the character's alignment isn't modelled).
  - **Beacon of Hope** (concentration, up to 1 minute, so only in a fight): the character has advantage on Wisdom saving throws ("advantage: Beacon of Hope"), and healing spells, potions and Divine Spark heal their most, with no dice drawn ("4 + 4 (Beacon of Hope: the most)"). Its death saving throws don't exist (ADR 0005). SRD 5.2's any number of creatures is one without companions.
  - **Bestow Curse** (concentration, up to 1 minute): the target makes a Wisdom saving throw or is cursed until the fight ends or the caster's concentration does. The curse is chosen at casting, from two (owner's choice): _attacks_, disadvantage on its attack rolls against the character ("disadvantage: Bestow Curse"); or _necrotic_, 1d8 necrotic more whenever the character damages it with an attack roll (a weapon, a spell attack or Spiritual Weapon; doubled by a critical hit) or a spell (Sacred Flame, Spirit Guardians and other damaging spells), shown after the damage ("plus 5 = 5 necrotic (Bestow Curse)"). Divine Spark and Sear Undead are no spells, so they don't add it, and neither does a Graze mastery's miss. The ability-disadvantage and forced-Dodge curses are omitted. A cast without a curse, or with another, is refused ("Choose the curse Bestow Curse lays: attacks or necrotic."), and so is a curse for any other spell. Its longer durations from 4th-level and higher slots are out of reach.
  - **Protection from Energy** (concentration, up to 1 hour, so until the next rest): resistance to the damage type chosen at casting, acid, cold, fire, lightning or thunder, cancelling a vulnerability to it. The bar offers it once for each type, in a fight or out of one, whatever the foes deal. SRD 5.2's touched creature is the character.
- **Casting outside a fight.** Only a healing spell, a spell whose effect outlasts a fight (Shield of Faith, Mage Armor, #337), or Guidance (#339), on the character, spending its slot; other spells are refused ("Fire Bolt is cast in a fight: outside one, only healing spells and spells that outlast a fight."; "Bless lasts no longer than a fight: cast it in one.").
- **Refusals.** The engine refuses, before any die: a spell that doesn't exist, a cantrip the character doesn't know or a spell it hasn't prepared (by name: "You haven't prepared Inflict Wounds."), a cantrip given a slot, a levelled spell without a slot of its level or higher, a slot level the character has none of or none left of, a second slot in a turn, its action or bonus action already spent, a damaging spell at anyone but a living opponent still fighting, and a healing spell at a foe or at full health.
- **Ongoing effects and durations (#337).** A buff spell puts an effect on its target, the caster or an ally (only the caster until companions): Bless adds a d4 to each attack roll and saving throw it makes, rolled after the d20 and named on the card ("9 + 5 + 2 (Bless) = 16"); Shield of Faith and Shield add to its AC; Mage Armor sets its base AC to 13 + Dexterity while it wears no armour (an unarmoured AC is 10 + Dexterity + a shield, so it adds 3), is refused on a target wearing armour and gives nothing if armour is put on later (SRD 5.2 ends it then). A spell already on its target can't be cast on it again, so no duration is renewed or extended. **Durations without a clock (D9):** up to 1 minute (Bless) ends when the fight ends; 10 minutes to 1 hour (Shield of Faith) ends at the next rest, short or long; 8 hours or "until dawn" (Mage Armor) ends at a long rest or when the adventure ends; Shield's one round ends at the start of the caster's next turn, or with the fight. Only a completed rest ends one: a rest the wandering encounter interrupts ends nothing. An effect that outlasts a fight is carried with the character's hit points and slots into the next fight; like them it lives only in the adventure, so none survives it. A spell lasting no longer than a fight is cast only in one.
- **The Wizard's spells (#340).**
  - **Ray of Frost** (cantrip): a ranged spell attack, 1d8 cold. Its slowing needs positions and is omitted.
  - **Chill Touch** (cantrip): a melee spell attack, 1d10 necrotic. Its target can't regain hit points until the end of the caster's next turn; no monster regains hit points here, so that has no effect.
  - **Shocking Grasp** (cantrip): a melee spell attack, 1d8 lightning, so no close-combat disadvantage from round 2. Its SRD 5.2 rider (no opportunity attacks) is omitted: there are no opportunity attacks.
  - **Chromatic Orb** (1st level): a ranged spell attack, 3d8, +1d8 per slot level above 1st, of the damage type chosen at casting: acid, cold, fire, lightning, poison or thunder. The bar offers it once for each type ("Chromatic Orb of fire (1st-level slot)"), and a cast without one, or with another, is refused ("Choose the damage type Chromatic Orb deals: …"). Its leap to a second creature needs positions and is omitted.
  - **Thunderwave** (1st level): a 15-foot cube, so up to 2 opponents (the cube heuristic under Areas of effect): a Constitution save, 2d8 thunder, half on a success, +1d8 per slot level above 1st. Its push needs positions and is omitted.
  - **Sleep** (1st level, concentration, up to 1 minute): a 5-foot sphere, so one target, which the bar offers at each foe as for a single-target spell. It makes a Wisdom save against the caster's DC, with advantage (#369, owner's house rule: an alert foe in a fight resists it), so it fails only when both d20s do; on a failure it is incapacitated until the end of its next turn, when it saves again, with advantage too, and on a second failure it is unconscious for as long as Sleep lasts, never running out by turns; a success on either save ends Sleep on it. Damage wakes it: the spell ends on it, with its condition. Sleep ends, and its conditions with it, when the caster's concentration does (another concentration spell, a failed Constitution save, falling) and with the fight. A creature immune to exhaustion (the bestiary's Zombie, Skeleton and Ghoul, as SRD 5.2 gives them) succeeds without a roll ("it is immune to exhaustion"). Omitted: an ally can't shake a sleeping target awake, as the character has no allies; and Unconscious lasts until the spell's minute ends, which is the fight (D9), not a count of 10 rounds.
- **The Wizard's spells (#343).**
  - **Ray of Sickness** (1st level): a ranged spell attack, 2d8 poison, +1d8 per slot level above 1st; a hit leaves the target poisoned (disadvantage on its attack rolls) until the end of its next turn, which stands for SRD 5.2's "until the end of your next turn" (the target's next turn comes first). A creature immune to poisoned isn't.
  - **Ice Knife** (1st level): a ranged spell attack, 1d10 piercing; hit or miss, the shard bursts and the target makes a Dexterity save or takes 2d6 cold, +1d6 per slot level above 1st (the knife stays 1d10). Its 5-foot burst catches only the target (D4); a target the knife drops isn't burst.
  - **Scorching Ray** (2nd level): three rays, one more per slot level above 2nd, each a ranged spell attack for 2d6 fire with its own d20 (round-2 disadvantage, a critical doubling its dice). The caster names up to as many different targets as there are rays, and the rays are split among them as evenly as they go, the first named taking any more: one target takes all three, two take two and one. SRD 5.2 lets each ray choose its target, so a split such as three and one of four rays isn't offered. A ray whose target is already down goes at the next target named still in the fight; with none left, the rest are lost. One slot and one action cast them all.
  - **Acid Arrow** (2nd level): a ranged spell attack, 4d4 acid; a hit also leaves acid that deals 2d4 acid at the end of the target's next turn (rolled then, not doubled by a critical), and a miss still splashes half the 4d4. Both grow by 1d4 per slot level above 2nd. The later acid is an effect on the target ("Acid Arrow takes hold on Bandit: 2d4 acid more at the end of its next turn"), gone once it burns or the fight ends.
  - **Mind Spike** (2nd level): a Wisdom save, 3d8 psychic, half on a success, +1d8 per slot level above 2nd. Knowing where the target is has no use without positions, so it keeps no concentration.
  - **Shatter** (2nd level): as the Cleric's (#338). **Hold Person** (#341): as the Cleric's; off this list from #348 until #369.
  - **Blur** (2nd level, concentration, up to 1 minute): attack rolls against the character have disadvantage ("disadvantage (Blur)") until its concentration ends or the fight does. No monster has Blindsight or Truesight to see through it.
  - **Mirror Image** (2nd level, 1 minute, no concentration): three illusory duplicates. Each time an attack roll hits the character, the engine rolls a d6 for each duplicate left; if any shows 3 or higher, a duplicate takes the hit instead and is destroyed, and the character takes no damage and nothing else the hit would do. The spell ends with its last duplicate ("Mirror Image ends on you: the last duplicate is gone.") or the fight. It comes after any reaction: a hit Shield turns into a miss rolls no d6s. No monster is blinded or has Blindsight or Truesight.
- **The Wizard's spells (#344).**
  - **Fireball** (3rd level): as under Spells as data (#338): a 20-foot sphere, so up to 4 opponents.
  - **Lightning Bolt** (3rd level): a 100-foot line, so up to 4 opponents (D4: 100 ÷ 30, rounded up): a Dexterity save, 8d6 lightning, half on a success, +1d6 per slot level above 3rd; the damage is rolled once.
  - **Bestow Curse** and **Protection from Energy** (3rd level): as the Cleric's (#342), against the Wizard's Intelligence save DC.
  - **Vampiric Touch** (3rd level, concentration, up to 1 minute, so until the fight ends): a melee spell attack as it is cast, 3d6 necrotic, +1d6 per slot level above 3rd; a hit heals the caster half the necrotic damage dealt, rounded down, up to its maximum ("Vampiric Touch: you regain 9 HP and have 29/32 HP."). While it lasts, on each later turn the caster's action (the Magic action, no slot) makes the attack again, at the same or another foe (`drain-attack`; refused without it: "You have no draining touch to make again: cast Vampiric Touch first."). It ends with the caster's concentration or the fight.
  - **Haste** (3rd level, concentration, up to 1 minute, so until the fight ends): on the caster (SRD 5.2's willing creature, without companions): +2 AC, advantage on Dexterity saving throws ("advantage: Haste"), and an additional action on each of its turns, the turn it is cast included, that can make one weapon attack only. The engine gives it as an Attack the turn's first attack takes outside an Attack action under way, so the action is left for a spell ("hasted" on the attack); Extra Attack's attacks still follow the action's. When Haste ends before the fight does (its concentration broken, another concentration spell cast, or being incapacitated), its holder is incapacitated until the end of its next turn, the rest of the current one too if it is its own: lethargy. Ending with the fight brings none. Its doubled speed and its Dash, Disengage, Hide and Utilize are omitted (no positions, no objects to use in a fight).
  - **Slow** (3rd level, concentration, up to 1 minute): a 40-foot cube, so up to 4 opponents (SRD 5.2's six are fewer here, D4). Each makes a Wisdom saving throw or is slowed while the caster concentrates, until the fight ends: −2 AC, −2 on Dexterity saving throws (in the save's bonus), and one attack a turn: a Multiattack makes one attack, and Rampage's bonus attack doesn't follow it. At the end of each of its turns a slowed foe saves again, ending it on a success ("Red Bandit makes a Wisdom saving throw against Slow: 2 + 0 = 2 against DC 15. Failure: still slowed."). Its halved speed, its bar on reactions (no monster reacts) and the chance that a slowed spellcaster's spell fails (no monster casts) are omitted.
- **Concentration (#337).** Bless and Shield of Faith need concentration. A caster concentrates on one spell at a time: casting another concentration spell ends the first ("Bless ends on you: you concentrate on another spell."). Damage that leaves it standing, in a fight or out of one (a trap's darts), calls for a Constitution saving throw against the higher of 10 and half the damage, rounded down, at most 30 (SRD 5.2); Bless's d4 counts. A failure ends the spell. Being incapacitated (paralysed) or falling also ends it. Each attack is one source of damage, its rider included, and calls for one save.
- **Area spells (#338).** An area spell catches up to its most opponents (see Rules that need positions), chosen by the caster, each once; more, a repeat, or an ally is refused before any die ("Burning Hands catches at most 2 opponents.", "Burning Hands can't catch the same creature twice."), and a spell without an area has one target ("Fire Bolt has one target."). The damage is rolled once ("Burning Hands: damage 4 + 5 + 6 = 15 fire, and each of its 2 targets saves against it."); then each target, in the order chosen, makes its own saving throw against the caster's DC and takes the full damage on a failure or half (rounded down) on a success, through its own resistance, vulnerability or immunity. A target it drops is defeated and its side checks morale before the next target saves. Unlike a single-target save spell, the damage is rolled even if every target then succeeds.
- **Reaction spells (#337).** Shield goes through the reaction path Uncanny Dodge uses, generalised: when an opponent's attack roll hits a caster that has Shield prepared, its reaction free and a 1st-level slot or higher left, the fight waits before the damage is rolled. The caster casts Shield, or takes the hit (a character with Uncanny Dodge too would be offered both, as one reaction). Shield's +5 AC counts against the attack that triggered it: a total that no longer meets the new AC misses, and a natural 20 still hits. It lasts until the start of the caster's next turn, so it also covers the attacker's other attacks that turn. SRD 5.2's other trigger, being targeted by Magic Missile, never comes: no monster casts.
- **The action bar and the AI DM.** The runtime projects a Cast action for each spell, slot level and target it would take now, disabled with its reason ("No slot", "Slot used this turn", "Full HP", "Already on", "Wearing armour"); a reaction spell only while a hit waits for an answer. In the browser (#337) the action bar's spell choice lists each spell at each slot level ("Magic Missile (1st-level slot)", "Fire Bolt (cantrip)") with a target choice ("Yourself" for a buff or healing spell) and Cast, disabled with the engine's reason; an area spell (#338) shows a box for each foe instead, "Up to 2", with the first ones ticked and the rest disabled once its most are, and Cast waits for at least one; outside a fight it offers what can be cast out of one, and answering a hit only reaction spells, beside Take the hit. The status strip lists each ongoing effect with the spell holding concentration and when it ends ("Bless, concentration, until the fight ends"), and the AI DM's character status and scene list them too. The command-line adapter lists the casts. The AI DM has a `cast` tool with `spell`, `slot_level` (null for a cantrip) and `targets` (a list, #338: one id, or an area spell's chosen opponents), offered only while some spell can be cast, listing the spells, slot levels, targets and each area spell's most the engine would accept; any other spell, slot or target, too many targets or one twice gets the engine's refusal. No tool takes a duration, and recasting an effect or keeping two concentration spells is refused or ends the first. The AI DM's `cast` tool also takes `damage_type` while Resistance is offered (#339), listing the types the engine would take. The balance harness casts only healing spells (#339; see Balance harness).
