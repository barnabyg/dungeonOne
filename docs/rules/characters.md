# Characters

## In scope

Ability modifiers, proficiency bonus, saving throws, skills, advantage and disadvantage, action, bonus action and reaction, the 5e XP table and SRD 5.2 stat blocks. Feats beyond the level-4 Ability Score Improvement, multiclassing, species and backgrounds' features other than the ability increase are not used.

## Where class rules live

A class is data. Each class's definition lives in `src/<class>-5e.ts` (`fighter`, `rogue`, `cleric`, `wizard`), in the shape `src/class-5e.ts` gives, beside what every class shares (abilities, skills, Fighting Styles, Weapon Mastery, the Ability Score Improvement). `src/character-5e.ts` builds, validates and derives every sheet from its class's definition and never asks which class it is; no class list outside the class registry names a class. Creation offers every class but a test-only one (`testOnly`): the Test Caster (`src/test-caster-class-5e.ts`) exists for tests and is never offered or gated. What each class does beyond its data is in [classes.md](classes.md).

## Creating a character

Every character is created at level 1 with 0 XP and full health, from its pending dice. The library refuses any other new sheet. The server validates every choice and projects every derived number; the page computes none.

- **Ability scores.** The engine rolls 4d6, dropping the lowest die, six times from a seeded stream of its own (SHA-256 of the browser's startup seed and the library's creation number). The rolls are saved in the library as a pending creation before the player sees them, so reloading, restarting the server with any seed or leaving the creation screen shows the same dice. There are no rerolls and no minimum set. The player places the six results freely; a fresh creation starts from its class's ability priority (`abilityPriority`), tied totals keeping roll order. Saving a character spends its dice; the next creation rolls a new set.
- **Deleting.** Deleting a character is permanent and removes only that character. A pending creation keeps its dice, so deleting is never a reroll.
- **Background increase.** After placement the player adds +2 to one ability and +1 to another, or +1 to three. No score can exceed 20. Backgrounds' other features are not used. Placement and the increase can change until the character is saved; the dice cannot.
- **Starting kit.** Each class offers named kits of common-tier gear and no starting coin. Kits are stingy by design: a character finds better gear in its first dungeons. One class's kits are of equal value within 3 gp (`KIT_VALUE_TOLERANCE`); kits of different classes need not be. Any legal loadout is a valid sheet.
- **Weapon Mastery.** A class that masters weapons chooses them at creation from the catalogue weapons whose mastery the game uses and that it is proficient with (see [classes.md](classes.md)). A mastery applies only while the character wields that weapon.

## Experience and levels

- **Earning XP.** XP is credited only when an adventure ends with the character alive, in a victory or an escape. It comes from each encounter won (the stat-block XP of the opponents defeated in it, partial XP for those that fled or surrendered, and any XP its module awards for sparing one; see [combat.md](combat.md)) and from the ending's XP. A defeat or an abandoned adventure credits nothing.
- **Naming opponents.** XP lines, cards and refusals name creatures with no article ("Defeated Kennel Mastiff; drove off Hesk's Hound"). Doors, traps, features and rooms keep "the".
- **Treasure.** Treasure is an item found by examining a feature, by searching an opponent's body once its fight is won, or offered by a surrendered opponent, and carried out alive. A fled opponent leaves no body, and what it carried leaves with it. A surrendered one keeps what it carried unless its module has it hand something over.
- **The ledger.** Each XP award and each find (treasure, coin or gear) is earned once per character. The sheet's ledger records them apart from what the character holds, and a find in the ledger is not there to find on a later visit, even once the character no longer holds it.
- **Levels.** Levels 1–5 follow the 5e XP table; XP above level 5's 6,500 is kept. The level follows the XP at once when the adventure settles, with its hit points and feature uses. Maximum HP is always derived from the current Constitution modifier, so a Constitution increase raises every level's.
- **Level-up choices.** A new level's choices are made on the character's sheet between adventures; the AI DM has no tool for them. They are the level-4 Ability Score Improvement (+2 to one ability or +1 to two, none above 20; other feats are not used), the Fighter's fourth weapon mastery with it, a caster's new prepared spells and cantrips, and the Wizard's spellbook spells and Scholar's Expertise. A sheet may lack an owed choice (fewer prepared spells than its level's count, say) only while it owes it. The sheet in the library records what is owed, so it survives a reload or restart, and the library refuses to start an adventure until every owed choice is made. A character that jumps from level 3 to 5 still owes its level-4 choice.

## Leaving, coin and settling

- **Leaving.** In a room that is a way out, out of a fight, the character may leave the adventure; the player confirms first. Leaving ends it in the module's escape-with-loot ending when the character carries treasure, coin found there or sold treasure found there, and in its escape-without-loot ending otherwise. The confirmation names that ending and its XP, unless already credited. When the ending falls short of the module's objective (its victory if it has one, or else getting out with loot) and the objective's award was not credited before, it also warns what is unmet, the award given up, and that the XP for fights won is kept. The warning comes from a fixed template; modules author no text for it. The AI DM has no tool to leave.
- **Coin.** Coin is SRD 5.2 copper, silver and gold pieces. A purse is held in copper and shown in mixed denominations; a new character has none. A module authors coin as an item of kind `coin`, hidden in a feature or carried by an opponent; the engine, not the AI DM, decides how much it holds. Taking it empties it into the purse at once, and the ledger records it as a find. Carrying coin found in the adventure out counts as escaping with loot, even once it is spent. Coin buys gear only from merchants inside adventures; there is no market between adventures.
- **Settling.** An adventure starts holding the character's possessions: its equipment, the treasure it holds and its purse. Settling a victory or an escape replaces the sheet's possessions with what the character holds at the end, adds its new finds and XP awards to the ledger, and rests it to full health. A defeat or an abandonment leaves the possessions, the ledger and the XP exactly as at the start, so coin and gear gained in an adventure are kept only if the character survives it. A character at 0 HP is defeated at once; there are no death saving throws (house rule), though a potion may save it as Last Stand (see [combat.md](combat.md)). Settling happens once, in the same library write that frees the character, so an interruption between the session save and the library write is settled on the next request without duplicating or losing anything.
- **Rest between adventures.** A rest between adventures restores every hit point, feature use (spell slots included) and hit die, so each adventure starts with all of them.
