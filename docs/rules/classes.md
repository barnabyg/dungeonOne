# Classes

Each class's definition in `src/<class>-5e.ts` (`fighter`, `rogue`, `cleric`, `wizard`) holds its numbers: hit die, saves, skills, training, masteries by level, features with their uses and recovery, ability priority, creation defaults, kits and, for a caster, its slots, cantrips, prepared counts and spell list. Creation, XP, level-up choices and rests between adventures are in [characters.md](characters.md); each spell's effect is in `src/spells-5e.ts` and the casting rules in [spells.md](spells.md); house rules are listed in [house-rules.md](house-rules.md). This file holds what the definitions don't say: engine abstractions, omitted features and house rules. Every class is offered at creation and plays levels 1–5.

## Fighter

- **Fighting Style** (level 1), one of four SRD 5.2 feats, each applying only with the gear it needs. The sheet says whether the style applies with what is equipped now.
  - _Archery:_ +2 to attack rolls with a ranged weapon, not to damage. Nothing is thrown without positions, so a dagger gets nothing.
  - _Defense:_ +1 AC while wearing body armour.
  - _Great Weapon Fighting:_ with a two-handed weapon, or a versatile one held in two hands (no shield, nothing else in hand), each 1 or 2 on a damage die counts as 3, critical-hit dice included.
  - _Two-Weapon Fighting:_ the light-weapon extra attack adds the ability modifier to its damage. It changes nothing else.
- **Second Wind** is refused at full health (house rule).
- **Weapon Mastery:** three kinds of weapon at creation, a fourth with the level-4 Ability Score Improvement. Graze, Nick, Sap and Vex are used. Slow needs positions and Cleave and Push are omitted; Topple (the quarterstaff's) is not offered, as no class masters a quarterstaff yet.
- **Tactical Mind** (level 2): right after the Fighter fails an ability check at a check site (a door, a search, a disarm, a topic, a feature), while it has a use of Second Wind left, it may add 1d10 to the check's total; the d20 is never rerolled. The check is graded again and the new band replaces the old one, so asking again is still refused; the site's outcome and, when the band changes, its words and effects follow the new band. What the failure already did, such as its damage, stands. The use of Second Wind is spent only if the check now succeeds. The offer lasts until the next accepted action, and is never made after a saving throw, a sneak, a lurk, a parley or Hide, which act on their result at once.
- **Champion** (level 3), its only subclass: Improved Critical (19–20) and Remarkable Athlete (advantage on initiative and Strength (Athletics) checks; its movement after a critical hit is omitted).
- **Extra Attack** (level 5): the Attack action makes two attacks, each at any living opponent, so the second may follow the first after its target falls. The second must come in the same turn or is lost. Action Surge gives a second Attack action with its own two attacks, and the light-weapon extra attack still follows (three in all, five with Action Surge). A Loading weapon fires once an action whatever the attacks the action allows.
- **Tactical Shift** (level 5) is omitted: it moves the character, and there are no positions.

## Rogue

- **Armour.** The Rogue is trained with light armour only. Medium or heavy armour, or a shield, costs it as SRD 5.2 says (see [gear.md](gear.md)). Its kits pack leather, a shortsword and thieves' tools, and are worth more than other classes' by design.
- **Expertise** (level 1): two skill proficiencies double their proficiency bonus. Expertise in Perception doubles it in passive Perception too.
- **Sneak Attack** (level 1) is engine-only: the engine applies it whenever its rules are met (a hit with a Finesse or ranged weapon, advantage without disadvantage, once per turn), and the player never asks for it; the AI DM has no tool or argument for it (house rule: it is never saved for a later attack). Advantage comes only from the engine's rules (Vex, a prone or paralysed target, Hide, Steady Aim and the like). Its dice are doubled by a critical hit and count in the damage a resistance halves. A miss, Graze included, deals none, and the turn's Sneak Attack is still to come. It may come from the light-weapon extra attack. The ally-adjacent clause is omitted until companions (see [house-rules.md](house-rules.md)).
- **Thieves' Cant** is flavour only.
- **Thieves' tools** proficiency adds the bonus to picking a lock or a tools disarm (see [exploring.md](exploring.md)).
- **Cunning Action** (level 2): Hide and Disengage, each a bonus action.
  - _Hide_ (abstraction): a Stealth check against the best passive Perception among the opponents still in the fight (10 for one without). On a success the Rogue is hidden: its next attack roll has advantage. Hiding lasts until it attacks or the fight ends, and a hidden Rogue can't hide again. A failure spends the bonus action. Untrained armour and the poisoned condition give the check disadvantage. Each Hide is rolled once and never rerolled by asking again. SRD 5.2's Hide needs cover or heavy obscurement, a DC 15 check and gives the Invisible condition; without positions or lighting it gives only the next attack's advantage, and foes attack the Rogue as usual.
  - _Disengage:_ fleeing later that turn draws no attacks (see [combat.md](combat.md)). It does nothing else, so it is offered only while the action a flight takes is left and some way out of the room is open. With none it is refused ("No way out"), and the AI DM is offered no `disengage` tool.
  - _Dash_ is omitted: with no movement it would do nothing.
- **Steady Aim** (level 3): a bonus action, offered while the Rogue still has an attack to make this turn, giving advantage on its next attack roll this turn; the advantage ends with that attack or the turn. SRD 5.2 allows it only if the Rogue hasn't moved; with no positions it never moves, so it can be used every turn. Steady Aim and Hide share the bonus action; a Rogue already hidden may still take Steady Aim, and one attack spends both.
- **Thief** (level 3), its only subclass:
  - _Fast Hands:_ in a fight, once the turn's object interaction is spent, the bonus action can draw, stow or swap a weapon again. Sleight of Hand checks and thieves' tools are used only outside fights, where nothing is timed, so there it changes nothing.
  - _Second-Story Work:_ there is no climb speed or jump distance. Instead a module marks a Strength check (Athletics or plain Strength) that climbs or jumps with `"movement": "climb"` or `"jump"`, and a Thief makes it with Dexterity in place of Strength, keeping its Athletics proficiency. Unmarked checks, such as forcing a door, use Strength.
- **Level 4** brings the Ability Score Improvement alone: the Rogue masters two kinds of weapon at every level.
- **Cunning Strike** (level 5): when the attack would deal Sneak Attack, the Rogue may give up Sneak Attack dice for one effect, chosen with the attack before the d20 is rolled (house rule). The dice go before damage is rolled (doubled on a critical hit), and the effect follows the damage. Saves are against 8 + Dexterity modifier + proficiency bonus.
  - _Poison_ (1d6): a Constitution save or poisoned for up to 10 of the target's turns (SRD 5.2's minute), repeating the save at the end of each. It needs no Poisoner's Kit, which the game doesn't have.
  - _Trip_ (1d6): a Large or smaller target makes a Dexterity save or is knocked prone, and gets up at the end of its next turn.
  - _Withdraw_ is omitted: it moves the Rogue.
  - An effect is offered only while the attack would deal Sneak Attack if it hits; a miss spends nothing. One effect per attack. Neither is offered against a target immune to its condition, nor Trip against a Huge or Gargantuan one.
- **Uncanny Dodge** (level 5), a reaction: when an opponent's attack roll hits, the fight waits before the damage is rolled, and nothing else can be done until the Rogue halves the damage or takes the hit. The total is halved once, rounding down. When the attack deals two types, each part is halved rounding down and any point that loses goes to the larger part (the weapon's on a tie): 7 slashing + 3 poison becomes 4 + 1. The reaction comes back at the start of the Rogue's turn, and taking a hit keeps it. A paralysed Rogue can't react. With no positions, darkness or invisibility, every attacker counts as seen.

## Cleric

- **Divine Order**, chosen at creation: _Protector_ (heavy armour and martial weapons) or _Thaumaturge_ (one more cantrip at every level, and its Wisdom modifier, at least +1, added to Arcana and Religion checks).
- **Kits** (#541) wear a chain shirt and a shield, as SRD 5.2's option A does: _Mace, chain shirt and shield_ (the default) or _Club, dagger, chain shirt and shield_, the dagger stowed. Both orders are trained with them (light and medium armour and shields): AC 13 + Dexterity (at most +2) + 2.
- **Prepared spells** change only between adventures (house rule); a new level's spells and cantrip are owed on the sheet (see [characters.md](characters.md)). Cantrips are never swapped.
- **Thaumaturgy and Light** are flavour only: never offered as a cast, and refused if cast. Light is on the list so a Thaumaturge, who knows the other four cantrips, has one to learn at level 4.
- **Channel Divinity** (level 2): two uses through level 5, a short rest restoring one and a long rest all. Each use is a Magic action against the spell save DC, only in a fight.
  - _Divine Spark:_ 1d8 + Wisdom modifier, either healing another creature on the character's side or making an opponent save on Constitution or take the total as radiant or necrotic damage, half on a success. It never targets the character, so without companions it has no healing target (single-player).
  - _Turn Undead:_ every undead opponent still in the fight and not already turned saves on Wisdom (no positions, so all are in range); one that fails is Frightened and Incapacitated for the fight. House rules: a turned undead repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, and its turning also ends when it is attacked (an attack roll or harmful spell aimed at it, hit or miss), as well as when it takes damage or the turner is Incapacitated or falls. A creature immune to both conditions isn't turned. Refused when no undead foe is left to turn. Every combatant carries its stat block's `creatureType`; every character is humanoid.
  - _Every foe turned_ (house rule): while every opponent still in the fight is turned, the character may attack them (ending that one's turning) or leave the room by an exit. Leaving keeps the fight unresolved: the fallen stay fallen, the standing ones keep their wounds and are met again unturned, and their XP comes only if the fight is later won. Effects lasting the fight end; the character keeps its hit points, slots and uses.
- **Life Domain** (level 3), its only subclass:
  - _Domain spells_ are always prepared and don't count against the prepared spells. A chosen spell that becomes always prepared leaves the choices, freeing the pick.
  - _Disciple of Life:_ a slot spell that restores hit points restores 2 + the slot's level more. Aid raises hit points rather than restoring them, and Divine Spark is no spell, so neither gains it.
  - _Preserve Life:_ a Channel Divinity use restores up to five times the Cleric level to the character while Bloodied, never above half its maximum. Without companions only the character is healed. Refused at half or more.
- **Level 4** brings the Ability Score Improvement alone, and a new cantrip.
- **Sear Undead** (level 5): each Turn Undead rolls d8s equal to the Wisdom modifier (at least one) once; each undead that fails its save takes the total as radiant damage, which doesn't end its turning. An undead immune to both conditions that fails still takes it.
- **The domain's level-5 spells** are omitted: Revivify needs a companion to revive (the character's own fall ends the adventure), and Mass Healing Word is off the list.
- **Spell list exclusions.** Aura of Vitality isn't a Cleric spell in SRD 5.2. Mass Healing Word and Beacon of Hope (#555) heal or help only the caster alone, worse than the spells kept. Protection from Energy (#555) resists acid, cold, fire, lightning and thunder, and no shipped foe deals them. Revivify, Dispel Magic, Remove Curse and the Cleric's other 3rd-level spells need companions, time, curses or enemy spells the game doesn't have.

## Wizard

- **Spellbook** (house rule): part of the sheet, not an item, so it can't be lost, sold or found. The Wizard prepares only spells in it. Each level after 1st writes two Wizard spells into it of a level it has slots for, owed on the sheet. A Wizard that gains two levels at once owes each level's two of a level its slots at that level allowed. Between adventures, a Wizard spell on a spell scroll it holds may be copied into it for 50 gp per spell level from the purse, if the spell is of a level it has slots for and not already in the book; copying uses up the scroll (see [gear.md](gear.md)). Ray of Sickness and Ice Knife are on the list so a level-2 Wizard always has two 1st-level spells to write.
- **Ritual Adept** is omitted: a ritual takes ten minutes more, and with no clock there is no time for it.
- **Arcane Recovery** (house rule: automatic): once per long rest, the first short rest that has a slot to regain also regains spent slots totalling up to half the Wizard level, rounded up, highest first, none of 6th level or higher, after the rest's own recovery. There is no choice to make and no AI DM tool. A short rest is offered at full HP while Arcane Recovery would regain a slot.
- **Scholar** (level 2): Expertise in one proficient skill among Arcana, History, Investigation, Medicine, Nature and Religion, owed on the sheet.
- **Evoker** (level 3), its only subclass:
  - _Evocation Savant_ is flavour only: its free Evocation spells are omitted.
  - _Potent Cantrip:_ when a damaging cantrip misses with its attack roll or its target succeeds on its save, the target still takes half the damage, rounded down, and nothing else. SRD 5.2 gives both cases; every Wizard cantrip is an attack roll, so the engine rolls the damage on such a miss. Levelled spells are unchanged.
- **Level 4** brings the Ability Score Improvement alone, and a new cantrip.
- **Memorize Spell** (level 5): when the Wizard finishes a short rest, it may swap one prepared 1st-level or higher spell for another in its spellbook. It is the one change to prepared spells during an adventure, and the swap lasts only that adventure: it lives in the session (`prepared`), and the library's prepared spells never change. Each short rest may swap again; an interrupted rest swaps nothing. A rest whose only gain is the swap is offered at full health.
- **Spell list exclusions.** At 2nd level, Misty Step, Web, Invisibility, Darkness and the rest of SRD 5.2's need positions, time or senses the game doesn't model. At 3rd, Counterspell and Dispel Magic have no foe spells to work on, Protection from Energy (#555) no foe dealing its types, and Fly, Blink, Gaseous Form, Fear, Stinking Cloud, Sleet Storm and the rest need positions, time or senses.
