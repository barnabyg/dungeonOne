# House rules

Rules that need positions are in [Rules that need positions](positions.md).

- A player character at 0 HP is defeated for good, apart from Last Stand's
  single death saving throw (below).
- **Last Stand** (#499): when an opponent's attack brings the character to 0 HP
  in a fight while it carries a Potion of Healing or Greater Healing and its
  reaction is free (and it isn't incapacitated), it makes one death saving
  throw: a d20 with no modifier, 11 or higher (an even chance; SRD 5.2's is 10).
  On a failure it falls. The Last Stand [setting](settings.md) makes the DC 11
  (the default) or 10, turns Last Stand off (the character falls at once) or
  makes it automatic (no death saving throw: the fall always waits). On a
  success the fall waits: the player drinks one potion with the reaction, rising
  to the potion's roll, or falls; with both kinds the player picks which. At 0
  HP its concentration ends and the hit gives no condition. Damage outside a
  fight (a trap, a check) offers none.
- **Rising Undead Fortitude:** each Undead Fortitude save a creature has made in
  a fight adds 5 to the DC of its next one (DC 5 + damage taken, + 5 after one
  rise, + 10 after two). SRD 5.2's DC never rises, so a light hit could leave a
  zombie standing over and over. A fight met again after the character left
  starts the count afresh.
- Second Wind is refused at full health, so a use is never wasted. The 2024
  rules allow it.
- At most one long rest per adventure, only at a module's rest site; SRD 5.2
  allows one a day anywhere safe. An interrupted rest takes no time and may be
  taken again. With the long rest [setting](settings.md) off (default on) no
  room is a rest site; a wand still regains its charges between adventures.
- At most two short rests per adventure (the default short rests
  [setting](settings.md): 0 to 4, or unlimited), each in a room with no
  unresolved hostile encounter; SRD 5.2 limits them only by time. A short rest
  takes no time, as there is no clock, and a rest that would restore nothing is
  refused.
- A Potion of Healing is refused at full health for the same reason, and so is a
  healing spell.
- Magic Missile's darts all strike the one target the spell names; SRD 5.2 lets
  them be split among targets. Their damage is added up and lands as one:
  resistance halves the total, and Undead Fortitude's DC counts it all.
- Guidance adds its d4 to the next graded check, whatever its skill, and then
  ends; SRD 5.2 adds it to every check with one chosen skill for the minute.
- Prepared spells change only between adventures, in the library; SRD 5.2 lets a
  Cleric change them after a long rest, and a Wizard after a long rest from its
  spellbook.
- A new level's spells are prepared on the sheet before the next adventure; SRD
  5.2 lets a Cleric prepare them after a long rest. A new level's cantrip is
  learned on the sheet the same way, and cantrips are never swapped; SRD 5.2
  lets a Cleric replace one cantrip on gaining a level.
- Spirit Guardians end with the fight, not at the next rest as the duration
  bands would give a 10-minute spell.
- Bestow Curse lays one of two curses: disadvantage on the target's attacks
  against the character, or 1d8 necrotic more from its attacks and spells.
- Leaving a room is allowed mid-fight only while every foe left is turned, or by
  fleeing; the fight stays unresolved, its fallen stay fallen, and its standing
  foes are met again, unturned, on coming back, with the hit points they had
  when the character left.
- Fleeing a fight draws one attack from each foe still in it and able to act,
  before the character goes, in place of opportunity attacks; SRD 5.2 gives an
  opportunity attack only to a foe the character moves out of reach of, with its
  reaction. A Rogue's Disengage avoids them. Foes never follow. With the fleeing
  draws attacks [setting](settings.md) off (default on), every flight draws
  none, as after Disengage, and Disengage is refused as doing nothing.
- Prayer of Healing's short rest gives back feature uses only, not hit dice, and
  doesn't count toward the short rests allowed.
- Arcane Recovery applies by itself on the first short rest that has a slot to
  regain; SRD 5.2 lets the Wizard choose when, and which slots.
- The Wizard's spellbook is part of the sheet, not an item: it can't be lost,
  stolen or sold. Spells are copied into it from scrolls between adventures (see
  [treasure.md](treasure.md#spell-scrolls-and-wands)). A new level's spellbook
  spells and Scholar's Expertise are chosen on the sheet before the next
  adventure; SRD 5.2 adds them as the level is gained.
- Evocation Savant is flavour only: its free Evocation spells are omitted.
- Memorize Spell's swap lasts only the adventure it is made in; the library's
  prepared spells are unchanged.
- Haste is cast on the caster only, its extra action makes one weapon attack
  only, and its lethargy follows any end before the fight's. Slow catches four,
  not six, and its riders on speed, reactions and spellcasting are omitted.
- Spell durations are bands, not minutes: up to 1 minute ends with the fight, 10
  minutes to 1 hour at the next rest, 8 hours at a long rest or the adventure's
  end. Mage Armor gives nothing while armour is worn rather than ending.
- Ability scores are rolled once with 4d6-drop-lowest and placed freely, with no
  rerolls, unless the ability scores [setting](settings.md) chooses another
  method.
- Starting gear is common tier only: each class's kits hold its weapons and, for
  a class that wears it, leather armour; one class's kits are of equal value
  (within `KIT_VALUE_TOLERANCE`), and there is no starting coin unless the
  starting coin [setting](settings.md) gives some.
- Sneak Attack is dealt whenever its rules are met; SRD 5.2 lets the Rogue
  choose. Without a choice of when to deal it, it is never saved for a later
  attack.
- Hide is a Stealth check against the opponents' best passive Perception, not
  SRD 5.2's DC 15, and gives only advantage on the next attack, not the
  Invisible condition (see [the Rogue](classes.md#rogue)).
- Cunning Strike is chosen with the attack, before the d20 is rolled, and
  applies only if the attack hits and deals Sneak Attack; a miss spends nothing.
  Its Poison needs no Poisoner's Kit (see [the Rogue](classes.md#rogue)).
- Uncanny Dodge is answered when the hit is known, before its damage is rolled.
- A character holds a second weapon only for the light-weapon extra attack, so
  both weapons must be light. Two weapons and a shield, or a two-handed weapon
  with a shield or a second weapon, are refused.
- A character always holds a weapon: its last one can be swapped but not
  unequipped, as unarmed strikes are not modelled.
- In a fight, one weapon change per turn, with the object interaction; armour
  and shields change only outside a fight.
- A character carries up to its Strength × 15 lb (the default
  [setting](settings.md)); taking or buying more is refused rather than slowing
  it, as there are no positions.
- Armour's stealth disadvantage gives disadvantage on the Stealth check to sneak
  in.
- Lurking opponents make one Stealth roll for the group, by the least stealthy
  of them; SRD 5.2 leaves a group's hiding to the DM.
- Morale, surrender, reaction rolls, parley, tolls and trade are house rules on
  top of 5e (see [talk-and-stealth.md](talk-and-stealth.md)).
- An opponent has its stat block's average hit points at the default opponent
  hit points [setting](settings.md); 75%, 125% or 150% scales them, rounded up
  and at least 1. Its XP is unchanged.
